Model evaluation

Cross-validation compares the models on training data; the held-out test set scores only the selected model.

Why accuracy can mislead. Only a minority of employees in the training data left, so a model that always predicts “stayed” is already right most of the time yet finds nobody at risk. Recall, F1, balanced accuracy and PR-AUC show how well leavers are actually identified.
