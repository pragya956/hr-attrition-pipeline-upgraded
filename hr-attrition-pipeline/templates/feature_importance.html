Feature importance

Which inputs the models rely on most. One-hot columns are mapped back to the original feature names.

Association, not causation. A high importance means the model uses a feature to separate leavers from stayers in historical data. It does not show that the factor makes anyone resign, and correlated features can share importance.

Permutation importance

How much ROC-AUC drops on held-out employees when one feature is shuffled. Bars show the mean; whiskers in the tooltip show the standard deviation over 10 repeats.

Random forest importances

Impurity-based, summed over each feature’s one-hot columns

Logistic regression coefficients

Sign shows direction; size is comparable because numeric inputs are standardised

How to read this. A positive coefficient raises the log-odds of leaving. Numeric features are per +1 standard deviation; one-hot features compare that category against all others. The odds ratio (ecoef) shows the multiplicative change in odds.
